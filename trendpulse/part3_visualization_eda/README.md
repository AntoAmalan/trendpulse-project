# Part 3: Visualization, EDA & Business Thinking

## Executive Content Strategy Briefing
* **Dominant Categories**: Entertainment and Music consistently drive the highest volume of trending views.
* **Velocity**: Videos that reach trending status typically do so within 2 to 4 days of initial publishing.
* **Retention Factors**: High engagement ratios (likes-to-dislikes > 95%) and active comment engagement separate lasting trending videos from those that vanish within 24 hours[cite: 6].
